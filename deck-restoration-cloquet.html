How It Works

Our wood restoration process

A proven four-step method designed for Cloquet's moisture, shade, and wood weathering.

STEP 01

Inspect

We assess your Cloquet deck, fence, or dock — wood type, staining level, and problem areas.

STEP 02

Treat

Wood-safe soft-wash solutions break down algae and mildew at the root — no destructive pressure.

STEP 03

Restore

A gentle low-pressure rinse lifts gray oxidation and green growth while protecting boards and rails.

STEP 04

Prep

Surfaces are left stain-ready — bright, clean wood prepared for sealant or your next project.

Why Soft-Wash Wood

Safe on wood.
Tough on gray weathering.

  • Never harsh blasting. Soft-wash wood restoration protects boards, rails, and fasteners — no splintering or furring.
  • Stain-ready surfaces. We remove algae and gray weathering so stain or sealant bonds properly on Cloquet decks.
  • Safer underfoot. Slippery green growth on wet wood is a hazard — restoration makes decks usable again.
  • Tuned for Cloquet. Designed for Carlton County's moisture, shade, and long freeze-thaw seasons.
