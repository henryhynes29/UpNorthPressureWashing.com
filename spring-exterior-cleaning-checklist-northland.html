The Order of Operations

If multiple items hit, sequence matters: gutters first (everything below depends on water going where it should), then siding and concrete (salt off the driveway, algae off the walls — one visit), then roof treatment (dead moss sheds over following weeks), then deck restoration in mid-summer when wood is dry enough to take stain. Bundling whatever you can into one truck visit is the biggest cost saver — details in the 2026 price guide.
