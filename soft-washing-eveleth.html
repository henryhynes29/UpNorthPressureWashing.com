Eveleth's Unique Challenge

Why Eveleth Homes Develop Algae & Mold Faster

Living near Lake Superior is beautiful — but the persistent moisture, heavy tree canopy, and brutal Northern Minnesota winters create near-ideal conditions for organic growth on your siding and roof. This isn't just cosmetic: unchecked algae and mold accelerate siding deterioration, trap moisture behind panels, and can cut years off your exterior's lifespan.

  • Shoreline humidity year-round. Lake Superior keeps ambient moisture elevated even in winter, feeding Gloeocapsa magma — the dark-streaking algae that colonizes siding and shingles.
  • Shaded hillside neighborhoods. Eveleth's tree-heavy terrain blocks sunlight on north- and west-facing walls, preventing the natural die-off that slows growth in sunnier climates.
  • Freeze-thaw cycles push growth in deep. Minnesota winters force organic debris into siding seams. By spring, buildup is embedded, not just surface-level — and won't rinse off with a garden hose.
  • Pressure washing accelerates the damage. High-pressure washing strips paint, cracks vinyl, and forces water behind siding panels. Worse, it doesn't kill growth at the root — algae returns within months.

Soft washing uses biodegradable surfactants at low pressure to kill algae and mold spores at their source. Results last 12–24 months — not a few weeks like a pressure rinse.

Most Eveleth homes stay noticeably clean for a full year to two years after treatment — dramatically longer than pressure washing, which rarely holds more than 3–6 months. Heavy shade may shorten this; south-facing walls often last even longer.

Backed by a 1-Year Guarantee

Every soft washing job we complete in Eveleth is backed by our regrowth guarantee. If algae or mold returns within 12 months of service, we come back and make it right — no charge, no questions. That's how confident we are in the process.

Best Time to Wash in MN

Late spring (after pollen season clears) or early fall gives the best results. Spring removes a full winter of buildup before it bakes in; fall prep protects siding through freeze-thaw cycles. Either timing keeps your home looking fresh year-round.
