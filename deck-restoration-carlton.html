How It Works

Our wood restoration process

A proven four-step method designed for Carlton's moisture, shade, and wood weathering.

STEP 01

Inspect

We assess your Carlton deck, fence, or dock — wood type, staining level, and problem areas.

STEP 02

Treat

Wood-safe soft-wash solutions break down algae and mildew at the root — no destructive pressure.

STEP 03

Restore

A gentle low-pressure rinse lifts gray oxidation and green growth while protecting boards and rails.

STEP 04

Prep

Surfaces are left stain-ready — bright, clean wood prepared for sealant or your next project.
