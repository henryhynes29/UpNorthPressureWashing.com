The Pressure Washing PSI Chart

Safe pressure, nozzle, and method for every exterior surface. Compiled by the crew at Up North Pressure Washing in Duluth, MN, from manufacturer specifications and a few thousand jobs in one of the harshest exterior climates in America. Free to reference or share — a link back to this page is appreciated.

Safe PSI by Surface

SurfaceSafe PSINozzle / MethodWhy it matters
Asphalt shingle roofNever pressure washSoft wash only (under 100 PSI, SH-based solution)Pressure strips granules and voids most shingle warranties. GAF, Owens Corning, and ARMA all specify low-pressure cleaning only.
Vinyl sidingSoft wash preferred; max 1,300–1,60040° white tip, downward angleHigh pressure forces water behind panels, causing hidden mold and warping. Never spray upward into laps.
LP SmartSide / engineered woodSoft wash onlyLow-pressure rinseLP's own maintenance guide warns pressure washing can void the substrate warranty.
Stucco / EIFS / DryvitSoft wash only (under 500)Wide fan, kept movingPressure pits the finish coat and opens hairline cracks that freeze-thaw cycles then destroy.
Wood deck (cedar, pine)500–80040° tip, with the grain, 12″+ standoffAnything stronger furs the grain and gouges soft spring wood. Cleaners do the work, not pressure.
Composite deck (Trex, etc.)Under 1,500 (check brand)40° fan tip, 8″+ standoffTrex allows up to 1,500 PSI; some brands void warranties over 1,000. Verify before you spray.
Brick & mortar500–1,000Wide fan, test a hidden spotOlder lime mortar erodes fast. Pre-1930 brick should usually not be pressure washed at all.
Natural stone500–1,500 (varies)Wide fanSandstone and limestone are far softer than granite. When in doubt, soft wash.
Concrete driveway / sidewalk2,500–3,500Surface cleaner + hot water for oil/saltConcrete is the one surface that genuinely needs pressure. Hot water is what releases oil and winter salt film.
Pavers1,500–2,500Surface cleaner, re-sand joints afterDirect high-pressure jets blow out joint sand and shift pavers.
Asphalt drivewayUnder 1,500Wide fan, cool waterHot water and high pressure both soften and scar asphalt binder.
Windows & screensNever pressure washPure-water pole or hand washPressure breaks seals on double-pane units — a fogged window is a replaced window.
Gutters (exterior)Soft washBrush + oxidation-safe cleaner for tiger stripingPressure won't remove electrostatically bonded striping; it just removes paint.
Painted wood siding500–1,20040° tip, test firstPre-1978 homes may have lead paint — chips must not be blasted into soil. Soft wash instead.
The rule of thumb: pressure is for concrete and stone. Everything else on a house gets cleaned by chemistry at low pressure — that's what soft washing is. If a contractor quotes you "pressure washing" for siding or a roof, ask exactly what PSI they'll use and what their insurance covers.

Northland Seasonal Exterior Cleaning Calendar (MN & WI)

Lake Superior weather is its own animal: 80+ freeze-thaw cycles a year, road salt from October to April, heavy June pollen, and lake-effect moisture that feeds algae on north-facing walls all summer.

SeasonWhat to clean & why
April–MayConcrete and garage floors — strip winter salt film before it accelerates spalling. Inspect siding for freeze-thaw cracks and winter mold growth.
JuneHouse wash after the pollen drop. Pollen is food for algae; washing it off now prevents the green film by August.
July–AugustRoof soft washing and deck restoration — surfaces dry fast and treatments cure properly. Best window for staining.
September–OctoberGutter cleaning after leaf drop, final window cleaning, and a siding rinse so organic matter doesn't sit on the house under snow for five months.
November–MarchExterior washing season is over — water freezes on contact. Book spring slots early; the post-thaw rush fills fast.
