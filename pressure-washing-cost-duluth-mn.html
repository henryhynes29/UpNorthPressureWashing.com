The 6 Things That Actually Change Your Price

1. Square footage — but less than you'd think

Twice the siding isn't twice the price. Setup, travel, and chemical mixing are fixed costs, so larger homes cost less per square foot. This is also why minimum service charges exist (ours is $650 for house washing): a crew, a truck, and 300 gallons of water cost the same to mobilize for a small job as a big one.

2. Buildup level

Light pollen film rinses fast. Established algae colonies on the north wall need dwell time, multiple applications, and brushing around trim. Heavy green-to-black growth can add 25–50% to a quote — which is the financial argument for washing every 2–3 years instead of every 8.

3. Stories and access

A second story adds ladder and reach time (gutter cleaning typically +50%). Steep lots — half of Duluth, in other words — slopes, retaining walls, and landscaping that needs protecting all add labor minutes that show up in the quote.

4. Surface material

Vinyl is the fastest to wash. Wood, stucco, and brick need gentler pressure, different chemistry, and more passes. See our PSI chart for why the method changes by surface — it's also a handy BS-detector for any contractor who says they'll "just blast it."

5. Roof pitch and walkability

A roof a technician can walk safely is one price; a steep North Shore A-frame that requires harnesses and roof anchors is another (+25% is typical). Nobody should be discounting their way onto a 12/12 pitch.

6. Season and scheduling

June–August is peak. Spring and fall slots are easier to get and contractors are more flexible on bundles. After mid-October, exterior washing in the Northland is done for the year — water freezes on contact.

How to Spot an Inflated — or Suspiciously Cheap — Quote

The Northland market has honest operators, weekend warriors with a rented machine, and a few outfits that price by what they think you'll pay. Three checks protect you:

Ask what's included. A real house-wash quote covers pre-soaking plants, hand-detailing around fixtures, and a guarantee against regrowth (ours is 1 year). A cheap quote that's "just spraying" isn't the same product.

Ask about insurance. $1M liability is the professional standard. An uninsured $300 house wash becomes very expensive when water gets forced behind your siding and the mold shows up in February.

Ask what pressure they'll use on what surface. If the answer for siding or shingles isn't some version of "low pressure / soft wash," walk away — high pressure on those surfaces causes damage you can't see until it's structural. (Roof manufacturers like GAF and Owens Corning specify low-pressure cleaning only; high pressure can void shingle warranties.)

5 Ways to Pay Less

Bundle services on one visit. The truck is already in your driveway — combining a house wash with gutters or windows is the single biggest discount lever in this industry. Book shoulder season (April–May, September–October). Wash on a 2–3 year cycle so you're always paying light-buildup prices. Coordinate with a neighbor — two adjacent jobs in one trip is worth asking about. And get a written quote — it forces precision and gives you something to compare.

FAQ

How much does it cost to pressure wash a house in Duluth?

A professional soft wash of an average home (1,400–1,800 sq ft of siding) typically runs $650–$975 depending on siding and buildup. Far-below-market quotes usually mean uninsured work or damaging high pressure.

Why are local prices higher than national averages online?

National figures blend in southern markets with 12-month seasons. Northland contractors earn a year's revenue in about six months, and our algae, road salt, and freeze-thaw grime are heavier than the national norm.

Is it cheaper to do it myself?

For concrete, yes. For siding and roofs, rented high-pressure machines cause much of the damage we're called to assess — the repair bill can exceed a decade of professional washes.

When's the cheapest time to book?

Early spring and late fall. Bundling services on one visit is the most reliable way to bring the total down at any time of year.
